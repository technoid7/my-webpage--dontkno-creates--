Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Who I am

This site is run by Oghenetega Onojeta (dontkno creates), a UI/UX designer based in Abuja, Nigeria. I’m responsible for the personal data described below.

What I collect

  • Nothing you type. The site has no accounts, contact forms or sign-ups.
  • Analytics data, only if you accept. If you choose “Accept analytics”, Google Analytics records things like the pages you view, your approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived. It uses cookies to tell visits apart.
  • Hosting logs. My host, Netlify, may log technical details such as your IP address and requested pages to deliver and secure the site.
  • Your cookie choice. I save your accept or decline choice in your browser’s local storage so I don’t keep asking.

Why and on what basis

I use analytics to understand which work and newsletter issues people find useful. I only do this with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer. Hosting logs are needed to run and protect the site.

Sharing and transfers

Analytics data is processed by Google, and hosting data by Netlify. These providers may process data outside Nigeria. I don’t sell your data.

How long I keep it

Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in my analytics account, and analytics cookies last up to two years unless you clear them sooner. See the Cookie policy for details.

Your rights

Under laws such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and the GDPR where it applies, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact me using the channels in the Contact section to make a request.

Children

This site isn’t aimed at children, and I don’t knowingly collect their data.

Links to other sites

I link to Behance, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Twitter. They have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies.

Changes

I’ll update this page when something changes and change the date above.
